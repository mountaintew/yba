Yba

Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

This policy applies to the Yba Chrome extension and to the Yba website at yba.lesterantonio.dev.

Summary

  • The extension does not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • The extension makes no network requests. Nothing that it reads leaves your browser.
  • The extension has no account and no sign-in.

What the extension reads

Yba runs only when you click its toolbar icon or press its keyboard shortcut. It then reads the page in the current tab: the text, the HTML structure and the computed styles. It uses this information to find design and copy issues and to show previews of fixes on that page.

Yba does not read other tabs, your browsing history, form input, passwords or cookies.

What the extension stores

When you approve a component, Yba saves these details in your browser with chrome.storage.local:

  • the address of the page
  • a CSS selector for the component
  • a short sample of the component's text and a copy of its HTML
  • the rules that you selected

This data stays on your device. Yba keeps it until you remove the component in the extension or uninstall the extension.

The report

When you click Copy for agent, Yba puts a text report on your clipboard. Yba does not send the report anywhere. You decide where to paste it.

Permissions

  • activeTab: gives Yba temporary access to the current tab, only after you open it.
  • scripting: adds the Yba panel to that tab.
  • storage: saves the components that you approve, on your device.

The website

The website has no analytics, no tracking and no cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. The Google privacy policy applies to that request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/mountaintew/yba/issues.
